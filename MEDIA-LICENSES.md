# Demo media licenses

The WAV recordings under `questions/media/**/clientFilesQuestion` were synthesized for this
repository from original demonstration text. The repository contributors dedicate those recordings
to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The `big-buck-bunny.m4v` files in the `video-basic` and `video-limited` question directories are
copies of the same six-second excerpt from _Big Buck Bunny_:

- Copyright © 2008 Blender Foundation, [bigbuckbunny.org](https://peach.blender.org/)
- Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- Source: the official 320×180 MP4 release from `download.blender.org`
- Change: transcoded to an Apple M4V/H.264 demonstration clip and trimmed to six seconds

The external-resource demos do not redistribute their media:

- `external-audio` loads the T. rex sample from MDN's `cc0-audio` collection.
- `youtube` embeds the official Blender Foundation upload of _Big Buck Bunny_, covered by the
  attribution and license above.
