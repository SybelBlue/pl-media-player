External audio URL

A direct HTTP(S) media URL uses the native player and supports its playback controls:

<pl-media-player url="https://interactive-examples.mdn.mozilla.net/media/cc0-audio/t-rex-roar.mp3" player-id="external-audio"> </pl-media-player>

Expected behavior: The remote MP3 loads with unlimited playback.

This demo loads media from an external source. PrairieTest and CBTF environments may block external sources, so the player may not load there.
I tested this demo.
