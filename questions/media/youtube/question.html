YouTube video embed

A YouTube watch URL is normalized and rendered as an embedded player:

<pl-media-player url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aqz-KE-bpKQ" player-id="video"> </pl-media-player>

Expected behavior: The official Big Buck Bunny video plays in a YouTube iframe. Native-player playback limits and restrictions are intentionally unavailable.

This demo loads media from an external source. PrairieTest and CBTF environments may block external sources, so the player may not load there.
